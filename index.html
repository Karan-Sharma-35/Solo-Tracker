    Tofu Bhurji (Low-Carb)

    Crumble 1 block firm tofu. Heat ghee, add cumin seeds, turmeric, and 2 tsp Deep Dry Garlic Chutney. Add tofu and salt. Cook 5-7 mins until moisture evaporates.

    Crispy Chilli Baby Corn (Low-Carb Mod)

    Boil 15 baby corn pieces, halve, and marinate in lemon juice. Coat in almond flour, then crushed cheese crisps. Pan-fry in butter until golden. Dust with chaat masala.

    Masala Idli (High-Carb)

    Toss frozen idli in a pan with minimal oil, mustard seeds, curry leaves, peanuts, and dry chili garlic powder.
